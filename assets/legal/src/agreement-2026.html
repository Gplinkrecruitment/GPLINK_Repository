As recruiters, we have one goal: ‘Matching the best GPs with the best GP jobs’
How do we do it?

Matching

We work to achieve a good match between a doctor and a practice. Our experience shows that a good match means doctors stay longer and work more effectively. We aim to present you with candidates that fit with the needs and culture of your practice as closely as possible. To achieve this, we advertise widely and filter applications to identify GPs who meet your requirements, therefore only presenting you with candidates who have a genuine interest in working with you. Historically, for every 3 interviews we schedule between you and the GP, at least 1 successful placement is made.

Contractual arrangements

If both the practice owners and the GPs express an interest in working together, GP Link will negotiate between the parties the terms of the contract and will assist in agreeing the contractual details. We aim to minimise the work required by the practice and aim to deliver a signed contract as quickly as possible. We encourage candidates to commit to ongoing placements to build relationships with a patient base.

Registration support for doctors based overseas

Most doctors sourced from overseas require extensive support for a period of 6–9 months as they work on arrangements to come to Australia. For most of this period, our GPs require our support several times a week to navigate the complex labyrinth of regulation as well as covering off everything they need to move to a new country. This support includes, but is not limited to:

  • Preparation of AMC/EPIC documentation
  • Liaison with the RACGP, AHPRA and Medicare, where we act as authorised agent of the doctor and deal with most issues that arise
  • Assistance with visas and migration (involvement of migration agents incurs an additional cost)

Difference

We make matches that are made to last

We want to place GPs in jobs where they stay for the long term because they are happy and engaged in the practice. Rather than making money by placing the same GP in clinic after clinic, we aim to find the best match so that doctors are more likely to stay longer term. We also rely heavily on referrals from our candidates, so we want to make sure they are 100% happy with our service.

We usually only submit each candidate to 2–3 practices. We don’t just send you a barrage of CVs and expect you to sort through the rest. We aim to send you only GPs that are interested in your GP job and who we think are a good match for your practice.

We only work in General Practice

We are not a recruitment agency that just dabbles in GP jobs but mostly works with hospital doctors. All of our recruitment and consulting work is 100% focused on General Practice. We are focused and committed to the provision of recruitment and business solutions for Australian GP Practices.

We support the whole process

We check that GPs have the right type of registration, and that they will be able to obtain a provider number to work in your practice.
