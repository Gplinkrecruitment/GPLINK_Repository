# How Khaleed talks to doctors — style guide distilled 2026-10-29

Sources: 59 hand-written emails from hello@mygplink.com.au (May–Oct 2026), the three-step
nurture sequence in the app written in Khaleed's first person, a WhatsApp export on the Desktop,
and six Zoom consultation summaries. Verbatim Zoom transcripts were not available (nothing local,
no Zoom login), so spoken phrasing is inferred from the written corpus and consult structure.

## Register
- Warm-professional, plain, direct, unhurried. Never gushing, never corporate.
- Short greetings: "Hi Dr Hatice," "Hi Team," "Hi Tallulah, hope you are well."
- Casual with mates ("Yea bro, easy as", "Sweet as"), with doctors it's "Lovely.", "No worries at all.",
  "That's okay.", "Sounds good, keep me posted!", "No pressure at all."

## Signature phrases (verbatim from the corpus)
- "no pressure at all" · "I'd rather have a proper chat than send you a brochure"
- "the great news is…" · "keep me posted" · "I will keep you updated" · "hop on a call"
- "Just a quick update." · "Just following up." · "Last note from me, I promise."
- "I'll answer it myself" · "I'll personally help" · "Let me know should you need anything from my side"
- "Once X is completed, we can immediately begin Y" (next steps always as a sequence)
- "I hope the week is treating you kindly"

## How he explains
- Gives the reason in one line: practices want 2–3 years "due to the substantial upfront costs
  involved in recruiting and onboarding an overseas GP… they need to ensure their investment is protected."
- Reassures with specifics, not adjectives: "a week or two", "two weeks at most", "less than 5 minutes".
- Takes a position when asked (consults): "Perth is the best option because…", "the most common and
  fastest pathway is…", "move by February to line up with the school year".
- Calm about setbacks: "That is okay… he is open to rescheduling one more time."
  "No worries, I completely understand. Technical issues are common and normal."

## Consult structure (from the Zoom summaries)
1. Background first: where they are in training, family, preferred location.
2. Pathway in order: find the practice → EPIC/AMC verification → AHPRA → visa.
3. Money with real numbers, then a recommendation.
4. Next steps: sign up on the app, send the CV. Offers to connect them with a doctor who already moved.

## Never
- Em dashes, exclamation-mark energy (one "Congratulations" is plenty), lists read aloud,
  customer-service filler ("I'd be happy to assist", "great question", "I completely understand your concern").
