# What 300 simulated UK GP calls on script v41 showed (29 Oct 2026)

300 calls (cases 0-299 of the UK-1000 suite), judged automatically; the failing transcripts were then read in full. 93 booked, 24 link sent, 212 judge fails.

## Lost bookings: 89 of 182 bookable doctors (49%)

| Cause | Calls |
|---|---|
| Deflection loop: refused any concrete answer ("I won't guess", "the consultant goes through that on the call") for up to six turns until the doctor gave up. Topics: fees 11, earnings figure 9, Khaleed's credentials 8, city/remote 6, English test 5, visa/process 5, part time 4, family 3, written proof 5. Eight of these were ended by Retell's loop detector. | 52 |
| Volunteered "he's not a doctor" about Khaleed, then could not answer the follow-up | 5 (inside 52) |
| Partner: accepted "I'll talk to my wife first", logged a callback or link instead of pencilling a movable slot | 8 |
| Doctor busy or driving, asked for a callback | 8 |
| Treated "maybe in a year or two" as a callback request, never gave the timeline | 5 |
| Refused 1 to 3 month trainees who should have been booked | 3 |
| Read out "the number we have on file" or "our records show"; doctor felt spied on | 3 (of 9 off-persona denials) |

Calls with three or more deflection turns: 43% of lost calls against 23% of booked ones.

## Scripted phrasing (3,023 agent turns)

Verbatim repeats across calls: the opener sentence (155), "is now an okay time" (158), "Speak soon, bye for now" (146, twice in 8 calls), "Which suits you better?" (136, re-asked in 15 calls), "What's got you looking at Australia?" (about 140). "I can't" opened 117 turns; "I won't guess" appeared 122 times; "fair enough / fair question" 62; "co-founder" (banned) 111; "does every consultation himself" (banned) 65. Two questions in one turn: 20 turns. Same question in other words: 39 calls.

## Brushed past

Doctors shared custody arrangements, a partner's job, burnout or a child's age and got "that makes sense" plus the next question (five quoted examples in the analysis directory).

## Trainee path (55 trainees finishing more than three months out)

Still booked or sent the link: 12 (22%). Mentioned the app sign-up: 30. Fully correct handling: 21 of 51. Voiced internal rules ("within our window", "we can only book within three months of finishing"): 70 calls in total across all persona types.

## Untrue or invented statements

"Director and co-founder"; "does every consultation himself"; "he's not a doctor" (unknown); "tomorrow evening's full"; "ten sessions Monday to Friday, weekends optional"; "Australian state schools are free for residents"; a 2003 MRCGP told it is "exactly the route we're looking for"; promises to email an overview or written terms that the tools cannot send; reading out the phone number on file; "I shouldn't have used that gp_status tool".

## Judge noise (fixed in criteria v2)

"Status never established" when the doctor had said they are a GP (7); honest AI disclosure penalised (4); hair-splitting on when the training check was asked (5); off-persona callbacks and denials graded as lost bookings (15); "realistic earnings" counted as a figure (3).

## Changes made in script v43 as a result

Straight-answers block with ranges for every topic doctors pressed on; "I won't guess" and "I don't have that" banned, "what I can do is" instead; after one practice-specific answer, one planning line and the offer, never a third round; partner handled by pencilling a movable slot with the partner on it; "not the right time" treated as a timing concern with a worked-back timeline; callbacks converted into booking the consultation itself for the suggested time; no phone numbers, "on file" or "our records"; Khaleed named only after the diary, "heads up the team", never whether he is a doctor; trainees 1 to 3 months booked like any GP, later trainees never get the booking link; wording varied call to call.
