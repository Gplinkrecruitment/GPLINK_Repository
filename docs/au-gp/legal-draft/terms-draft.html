For doctors who trained overseas, GP Link also provides:

4. How Introductions Work

GP Link acts as an independent recruitment intermediary between doctors and practices.

  • You choose. We introduce you to a practice only after you have agreed to be put forward to that practice. We ask each time, for each practice. [LEGAL REVIEW 9]
  • What the practice receives. When you agree, we send that practice your name, a short introduction, your CV and any cover letter, and confirmation that we have checked your registration. Our Privacy Policy sets out the detail.
  • Your current employer. We do not contact your current practice or employer, and we do not tell them you are speaking to us. [LEGAL REVIEW 8] If you ask us not to introduce you to a particular practice or group, tell us and we will not.
  • Limits on applications. We may limit how many roles you can apply for or interview for at one time, so that practices hear only from doctors who are seriously interested.

Any employment or contractor agreement is made directly between you and the practice. GP Link is not a party to it.
