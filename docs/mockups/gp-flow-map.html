The screening question

Decides everything

One rule governs the rest of the funnel: are they a registered GP, and are they in the UK, Ireland or New Zealand? ("Northern Ireland" is deliberately read as UK, not Ireland.)

✓ Qualified

They get a private token — the magic link that recognises them later. The calendar opens. Chasing emails switch on.

✕ Screened out

Politely turned down. No token, ever — which also means they can never be chased. A dead end by design.

Proven

Which jobs they're allowed to see

The DPA rule

Jobs in a district of workforce shortage are open to everyone. The rest are for Australia-trained GPs only — and an overseas-trained GP sees those as blurred placeholders, not hidden. They know something is there; they just can't read it.

Blurred as
"GP Opportunity" · "Confidential practice" · state only · "You don't currently qualify for this role."
Safety
Fails closed — if anything errors, every job blurs rather than leaking a practice name

What's actually wrong

The honest list. Each item was read out of the code or proven against production today.

Fixed

The "Start MyIntealth" button went to a dead page

A misspelled address in the first email after onboarding. Every GP hit "Not found" on their next step. Fixed today, with a test that guards every email button against the same mistake.

Needs you

The Facebook door is bolted shut

The lead webhook answers 503 because its secret isn't set. Until you add the three settings in Vercel and point Meta at the webhook, no lead can enter that way. Ads that send traffic straight to /start work today.

Needs you

Nobody has ever clicked /start in a real browser

I can prove the page serves, that the security policy permits Calendly, and that every underlying call works — but I have no browser in this session, so the calendar has never been seen loading. Worth five minutes before you spend on ads.

Open

Sign-ups don't tell the lead record

Conversion is noticed later by the hourly cron rather than recorded at sign-up. Anyone who unsubscribed and then signed up is never counted — so your funnel numbers will under-report.

Question

An offer can go out with no interview

Nothing in the code requires an interview before an offer, and a GP can be placed without one. This may be deliberate — flagging, not changing.

Cosmetic

Three stale comments and one dead button

The code still calls this a "7-stage journey" (it's 6), the onboarding chase emails name the wrong step numbers, and the wizard's SKIP button can never appear. None of these affect a GP today.
