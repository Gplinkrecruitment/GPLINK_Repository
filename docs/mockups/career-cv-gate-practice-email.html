Candidate submission email to the practice

What SOP Medical Centre would receive. The summary sentences are built from the GP's onboarding answers (country, pathway, hoped start date); the recommendation is AI-written from their verified CV; the attachments are the careers CV + cover letter — never registration-file documents.
