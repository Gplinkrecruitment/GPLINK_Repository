Interviews are covered. The consultation call is not.

The interview reminder cron sends the GP an email and a WhatsApp with the Zoom link at 24 hours and 1 hour; the practice gets an email (no WhatsApp — we don't store a practice phone number). But the consultation reminder is staff-facing: it reminds the RSO, not the GP, and a /start booking has no RSO assigned, so nobody is reminded at all. Calendly sends its own confirmation and reminder to the doctor (the Zoom link rides in the calendar invite), but that's Calendly's generic email — the app itself sends the consult booker nothing, and there's no WhatsApp.

Email transport is Resend (marketing + transactional) and the Gmail API (AHPRA/SPPA threads). WhatsApp is DoubleTick. Recipients: GP = the doctor, practice = the clinic contact, RSO = the assigned consultant, ops/owner = your inbox, officer = the AHPRA case officer.

Marketing sends (consult & onboarding nudges, admin nudge) carry an unsubscribe link and honour the suppression list. Everything else is transactional.
