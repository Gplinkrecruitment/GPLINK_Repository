You can also combine them — e.g. B as the overview with C's detail opening when you click a row.

A

Map view with lines

Click a centre → lines light up to its GPs. One position in focus at a time.

B

Radar rows (recommended)

Every position + its matches visible at once, urgency-sorted, statuses on each GP node.

C

Split view

Compact list + deep detail pane. Most room for actions, least visual.

D

B + C combined

Radar rows as the glanceable overview; clicking a row opens the full detail pane with actions.
