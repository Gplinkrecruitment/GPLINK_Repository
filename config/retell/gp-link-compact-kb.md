# GP Link: compact reference for phone calls with UK GPs

## Who GP Link is
GP Link places UK-trained GPs into Australian general practices and handles registration, visa and the job end to end. It is free for doctors because practices pay GP Link. GP Link works with about 300 practices across every Australian state. Consultations are run by GP Link's consultants.

## Who can book a consultation
A consultation can be booked for qualified GPs (MRCGP and CCT) and GPs finishing training within three months. GPs finishing training more than three months away are not booked: they get the GP Link app sign-up link by email and a call about a month before they finish. Hospital doctors, registrars and consultants who are not GPs are not booked.

## The fast route (expedited specialist pathway)
The expedited specialist pathway is the Medical Board of Australia's fast-track list. It covers MRCGP awarded from August 2007 onwards, because the current MRCGP exam format began in August 2007. It gives specialist registration with AHPRA directly, with no RACGP fellowship exams. The first six months are supervised and full time. It takes about six months from first chat to seeing patients, and finding a practice takes the first two to three months.

## The slower route (comparability)
An MRCGP awarded before August 2007, and GP training completed outside the UK, Ireland or New Zealand, go through the comparability route assessed by the RACGP. It usually takes around nine months and is never promised.

## Process step by step
1. Sign up on the GP Link app and send a CV.
2. GP Link sends the CV to practices that fit the doctor's preferences.
3. The doctor picks from the offers and meets the practice on a video call.
4. The doctor signs a contract with the practice.
5. AHPRA registration, then the employer-sponsored visa.

## English test
AHPRA requires a recent English test, and the doctor must have worked within twelve months of sitting it. An OET or PLAB older than that usually means sitting a fresh test.

## Location
Nobody is posted anywhere. Doctors only receive offers in areas they choose and they pick the practice. Overseas GPs start in a Distribution Priority Area, which is often not remote: there are many near Newcastle, the Central Coast, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth. Perth suits families with relatives in the Middle East or South Asia.

## Visa, family and permanent residency
Most doctors start on an employer-sponsored work visa sponsored by the practice. The spouse and children come as dependants on the same visa, and the spouse has full work rights. Teachers and nurses also need registration with their state board. It is typically about two years on the work visa before applying for permanent residency, and about four more years to citizenship; this is never promised. A registered migration agent or immigration lawyer lodges the visa. GP Link and its consultants are not migration agents.

## Costs of moving
The practice usually pays for visa sponsorship. The migration agent's fee is often split between the practice and the doctor. Most practices offer a relocation package. All costs are written into the doctor's contract with the practice before they sign.

## Pay
GPs in Australia are paid a share of what they bill, not a salary. Most keep around seventy percent of billings, and regional areas pay more. Practices guarantee a minimum income for the first few months. Specific income figures are only given by the consultant on the consultation.

## Work pattern
Full time is about ten sessions a week, Monday to Friday, with optional weekend sessions that pay more. Part-time is possible after the first six supervised months. Skin checks, minor surgery and dermatology need no extra certificate and are in high demand.

## The consultation
The consultation is a free, thirty-minute Zoom call with a GP Link consultant, with no obligation. It covers the doctor's registration route, the visa and the family side, and realistic earnings for where they want to be. After booking, the confirmation and Zoom link go out by email and WhatsApp, with reminders the day before and a few minutes before. The diary shows times over the next two weeks. The invite goes to one email address. The website is mygplink.com.au.
