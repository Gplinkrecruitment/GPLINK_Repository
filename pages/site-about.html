About GP Link

We move GP careers to Australia

GP Link is a specialist recruitment and registration service for overseas-trained GPs. Free for doctors, end to end.

Why we exist

A maze, run for you, not by you

Australia needs GPs, especially in outer-metro and regional communities, where the shortage is most acute. At the same time, overseas-trained doctors face a genuine maze to get here: AHPRA registration, AMC verification, English requirements, visa sponsorship and Medicare provider number rules, each with its own process and its own way to go wrong. GP Link exists to run that whole pathway for you, so a qualified doctor can actually arrive and start work, not just receive a job offer on paper.

Our service is completely free for doctors, always. Practices engage GP Link to find, vet and register the GPs they need, which means that as a doctor, you never pay us a fee, and you get the same team managing your registration, visa and relocation from your first call through to your first day.

What we handle

One team, every step of the move

Registration & AHPRA

AMC verification, your AHPRA application and English requirements, all managed and tracked for you.

Visa & migration

Employer-backed sponsorship coordination for you and your family, handled alongside registration.

Placement & contracts

Matching to the right practice, interviews arranged and offers negotiated on your behalf.

Relocation & settling in

From booking flights to your first day at the practice, we help you and your family settle in.
