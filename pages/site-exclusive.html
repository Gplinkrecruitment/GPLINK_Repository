Why some roles never go public

Practices come to us to fill roles privately

A lot of practice-owner recruitment simply never reaches a public job board. Practices often prefer a quiet, managed search — over an open ad that brings unqualified enquiries, tips off staff or competitors, or takes months to sort through. So they come to GP Link and ask us to handle it discreetly instead.

Those roles aren't posted anywhere public — including on this website. The only way to be considered for them is to be inside the GP Link app, where your dedicated Registration Support Officer can match you directly.

Create a free account and your Registration Support Officer will match you to exclusive opportunities that fit your skills, visa status and the life you want to build — then you choose which ones you actually want to apply for. Nothing is applied for on your behalf without your say-so.
