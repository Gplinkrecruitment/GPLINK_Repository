What we talk about

Three things most GPs would change

You already know how to be a GP in Australia. The conversation is about where, and on what terms.

Billing mix

Bulk billing, mixed or private. Tell us which suits the way you practise, and we look for practices that work that way.

Location

Closer to home, a different suburb, a different state. Tell us where you want to be. If anything limits where you can work, we go through your position with you before we suggest a practice.

Flexibility

The sessions you work, the days you keep free, room for a special interest. You tell us what the week should look like.

How it works

From a first conversation to a first day

Six steps, and you decide at each one whether to take the next.

  1. 1Talk to usBook a 30-minute call and tell us what you would change about where you work now.
  2. 2We check your registrationWe confirm your Ahpra registration on the public register. There is nothing for you to do.
  3. 3You choose who we introduce you toWe suggest practices that fit. Your profile goes to a practice only when you say yes to that practice.
  4. 4Meet the practiceIf the practice would like to meet you, you choose a time and we set up the video call.
  5. 5The offerIf the practice makes an offer, you read it in your own time, and take your own advice if you want it.
  6. 6Start after your notice periodYou give notice when you are ready. We agree the start date with you and the practice around it.
Confidential by default

What we do not do without asking you

Looking at other roles while you are working is normal, and it is your business. So nothing leaves our hands until you say so.

  • Contact your current practiceWe never contact your current practice without asking you first.
  • Send your profile to a practiceA practice sees your profile only after you agree to share it with that practice for that role. You are asked each time.
  • Contact a referee at your current practiceIf one of your referees works where you work now, tell us, and we check with you before we get in touch with them.
  • Apply for a role in your nameYou see the role and you decide. Nothing is put forward for you without your agreement.
