Two ways to hire

A GP who is already here, or one who is moving here

Which track suits your vacancy depends mostly on where your practice is. We check that with you before we introduce anyone.

Local track

GPs already registered in Australia

Doctors who hold Ahpra registration today and are looking for a practice that suits them better.

  • No visa and no registration wait. An Australian vocationally registered GP can start once they have worked out their notice with their current practice.
  • For GPs without location or supervision restrictions, your practice does not need to be in a Distribution Priority Area and there is no supervision arrangement to set up.
  • Before we introduce a doctor we check their registration on the public Ahpra register and whether any location or supervision restriction applies to them.
  • You tell us on your practice profile which GPs you will consider. A vocationally registered GP is the default.
Overseas track

Overseas-trained GPs moving to Australia

Doctors from the UK, Ireland and New Zealand who have committed to practising here.

  • We run AHPRA, AMC and the visa with the doctor, so they arrive ready to work.
  • These doctors generally need a practice in a Distribution Priority Area to bill Medicare. We check your location first.
  • Timing depends on each doctor's registration and visa pathway, and we give you a realistic timeframe up front.

We introduce the doctors we have, and we cannot promise that a GP is available for a particular suburb at a particular time. If we have nobody suitable for your vacancy we will tell you so.
