Your recommended pathway to permanent residency in Australia.

Your Pathway to Permanent Residency

As a GP placed through GP Link, your most direct pathway to permanent residency is through the Subclass 482 temporary visa followed by the Subclass 186 permanent visa. Your GP Link agent will guide you through each stage.

1
Temporary Skill Shortage
Subclass 482 Visa
Your sponsoring practice nominates you for a temporary work visa. This allows you to live and work in Australia for up to 4 years while you build towards permanent residency.
2
Transitional Stream
Subclass 186 Visa
After working with your sponsor on the 482 visa, you become eligible for permanent residency through the Transitional Stream. Your sponsor nominates you and you apply for the 186 visa.
Destination
Permanent Residency
With a granted 186 visa, you gain permanent residency in Australia with full work rights, Medicare access, and a pathway to citizenship.
More information will appear here once you reach the visa step in your registration journey. Your GP Link agent will manage the process and update you at each stage.
