Your pathway to permanent residency 482 → 186 → citizenship

Based on our team's review of your CV and current GMC registration, the most streamlined pathway to permanent residency for you is through the Subclass 482 temporary visa followed by the Subclass 186 permanent visa. Your Registration Support Officer will guide you through each stage.

Permanent Residency · Transitional Stream
Subclass 186 Visa
After 2 years with your sponsor on the 482 visa, you become eligible for permanent residency. Your sponsor nominates you and you apply for the 186 visa through the Transitional Stream, granting full work rights and Medicare access.
4 years

Final Destination
Australian Citizenship
